## 0.1.0
- First release.
- Ask Hermes for an xStocks trade and review it in a clear window: price, fees, a simple risk check and your daily limit.
- Approve or reject in Hermes Desktop, or with `/greenlight` in the terminal and chat apps.
- After you approve, a link (and a QR code for your phone's wallet) opens the trade in your own wallet. Greenlight never holds your keys.
- Optional limits: daily, per-trade, allowed stocks and price-change tolerance.
- A tamper-evident history of every decision.
- Practice trades that need no wallet.
